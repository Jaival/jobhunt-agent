# Upgrading from v0.4.0 to v0.5.0

v0.5.0 ("grounded & modular") checks every letter against your own files before it builds, and tailors each job
in its own fresh context. Your CV, letters and config.yaml settings don't change, but **a letter that passed
before can now fail the build** when it claims something your files don't back up.

Older engine? Go to [v0.4.0](v0.4.0.md) first; "update the engine" does both steps in one go, but read both
guides for what changes.

## Before you start
- Your scheduled task must still be a local Code-tab task in permission mode Auto (see [v0.3.0](v0.3.0.md)).
- Close any running scheduled run (the update changes `.claude/`).
- About 10 minutes. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## How to update
Open the workspace folder in Claude Desktop's **Code** tab and say:

```text
update the engine
```

Claude follows [UPGRADING.md](../../UPGRADING.md). What is new for v0.5.0:

| Step | Command Claude runs | What it means for v0.5.0 |
|---|---|---|
| 2 Look | `python engine-new/tools/upgrade.py status` | `engine 0.4.0` and `engine version on GitHub: 0.5.0`. |
| 5 Settings | `python engine/tools/upgrade.py migrate` | **The v0.5.0 step.** config.yaml stays at version 2. It copies `.claude/agents/tailor.md` (the tailor) and four skills into `.claude/skills/` (`ats-review`, `update-engine`, `remap-cv`, `onboarding`). |
| 6 Check | `python engine/tools/setup_check.py --init` | Must say READY. |
| 7 Prove | `upgrade.py compare` | Builds your recent applications with both engines. A letter with a number or tool that isn't in your CV, CLAUDE.md, projects.md, cv/variants.yaml or the job's ad now shows as **FAILS with the new one**, with the terms. That's the new grounding check doing its job: correct the claim in that application.md if you still want to send it. Otherwise expect **SAME DOCUMENTS**. |

Then click **Run now** on the scheduled task once and check the brief (below).

## What changes in the daily run
- **Letters are checked against your files.** Every number and every skill or tool term in a letter or the CV
  headline must appear in your master CV, CLAUDE.md (not its "Gaps" section), projects.md, cv/variants.yaml or
  the job's `ad.json`. An invented metric ("increased traffic by 40%") or a tool you only list under Gaps fails
  the build with the list. The routine corrects the letter and rebuilds; it never adds facts to your files to
  make a letter pass. A term only the ad has passes with a "from the ad only" line, and the letter must name it as
  something to learn. `grounding_ok: [term]` in application.md lets a reviewed exception through.
- **One tailor per job.** Each selected job is tailored by its own `tailor` subagent, one after the other, so one
  job's ad, letter or build errors can't carry over into the next. The brief looks the same; the tailor's
  `grounding` and `actions` lines go under "Your actions".
- **The on-request workflows are skills.** "run the ATS review", "update the engine", "remap my CV" and the setup
  interview work with the same phrases as before.
- **The ledger keeps sub-scores.** `data/seen_jobs.csv` gets the columns `subscores`, `engine_version` and
  `model` on its next save; older rows stay blank. The Friday review compares them with outcomes.
- **University name variants.** config.yaml `university` can be a list (`[Universität Mannheim, University of
  Mannheim]`); a student letter passes when it names any of them.

## Check that it worked
After the first **Run now**:
1. The brief's Sources section shows `engine 0.5.0 (...)` and `guard active: ...`.
2. Each tailored job's folder has job.md, application.md and the built documents; the brief lists any
   `grounding` terms under "Your actions".
3. The newest rows in `data/seen_jobs.csv` have sub-scores like `skills=4 seniority=5 ...` and `0.5.0`.

## Going back
`python engine/tools/upgrade.py rollback` puts the old engine and config.yaml back. `.claude/agents/tailor.md` and
`.claude/skills/` stay and do nothing with v0.4.0 (its ROUTINE.md still holds the on-request workflows). A
`university` list must go back to one name.
