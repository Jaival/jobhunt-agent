# Skills I confirmed

The routine asks about terms from job ads that are in none of my files ("Do you know Agile?"). My answers go
here, one row per skill, so it never asks twice. `python engine/tools/keywords.py fact "<skill>" yes --note "..."`
adds a row; editing the table by hand works too.

- **yes**: I can show it. The note says where (job, project, course). Letters and CVs may claim it.
- **learning**: I know the basics or am learning it. Letters may only name it as something I'm learning.
- **no**: I don't have it. Never claimed.
- **Aliases**: other spellings an ad may use, comma-separated.
