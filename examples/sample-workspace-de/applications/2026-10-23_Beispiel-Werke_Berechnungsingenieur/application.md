That is the work I did for a year and a half as a working student at Muster Automotive GmbH in Regensburg, and I would like to continue it with you in Nuremberg.

At Muster Automotive I prepared strength assessments for brackets in ANSYS Mechanical. I learned to state my assumptions openly and to question results before they go into a release.

Your ad mentions Abaqus and non-linear contact analysis. I used Abaqus during my studies, but not yet for large contact models. I want to close this gap in my first weeks so that I can take complete assessments off your team's plate soon.

I can start immediately and would gladly move to Nuremberg for this role.
